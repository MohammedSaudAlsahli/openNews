# OpenStudio 0.2.1 — Experimental preview

Smaller download, clearer live activity, and more reliable news discovery. **Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later.**

## What changed

- Reduced the application bundle by removing bundled PyTorch/Sentence Transformers. News, RSS, article reading and keyword search work immediately without downloading a model. Optional semantic search uses an Ollama embedding model selected in Settings.
- Live activity shows the available public thinking summary and the current search or source. It collapses when the reply finishes and remains available with the saved reply.
- New thinking summaries have Arabic and English versions; the displayed version follows the request language. Translation is coalesced in the background and may add provider requests and latency. Availability depends on the provider returning a public summary; failures are shown explicitly.
- RSS.mobi discovery searches its complete OPML directory instead of stopping at 60 feeds. Followed sources include manually added subscriptions such as subreddits, even when absent from the directory.
- Support for subreddit feeds and X accounts through a user-provided RSS conversion feed. No X API account is required for the conversion-feed option.
- More consistent article-summary language, repair of mismatched cached summaries, and clearer background work status.
- Simpler settings and model selection, compact conversation activity, less redundant chrome, and smoother text updates.

## Download and validation

DMG: **299.6 MB** (285.7 MiB), around 56% smaller than v0.2.0. Build **202**.
371 engine tests and 53 native app tests passed locally; the signed relocated bundle, conversation smoke tests and DMG checksum verification passed. See SHA256SUMS.txt in the release assets.

## Install or update

Download **OpenStudio-0.2.1-macOS-arm64.dmg**, open it, and drag OpenStudio to Applications.
Existing users can use **Check for Updates**; the signed Sparkle feed is published after the download is available.
Saved news, drafts, conversations, connections and downloaded models remain outside the app bundle.
Python and the news runtime are bundled; local models are optional downloads.

**Experimental: not Apple-notarized and not Developer ID signed.** This is an ad-hoc signed preview with a separately signed Sparkle update. macOS may block the first launch. Follow [the setup guide](https://github.com/MohammedSaudAlsahli/openStudio/blob/v0.2.1/docs/SETUP.md); do not disable Gatekeeper.

## Known limits

- Intel Macs are not included. No automatic publishing to social networks.
- Model quality and public-summary availability vary by provider. Translation is not a view into private internal reasoning. Old saved summaries are not retroactively translated.
- Local inference remains subject to thermal and Low Power Mode limits. Bilingual summary translation for local providers may wait for the device inference slot.
- RSS.mobi and third-party feeds are external services; unavailable feeds show errors. Reddit has announced the retirement of its RSS feeds on November 13, 2026.

## التغييرات بالعربية

حزمة أصغر، عرض مباشر وواضح للنشاط، ونسختان عربية وإنجليزية للملخصات الجديدة حسب لغة الطلب. أُصلحت حدود دليل RSS.mobi واختفاء بعض المصادر المتابعة، وحُسّنت الإعدادات وتناسق لغة تلخيص المقالات. الأخبار تعمل فور التثبيت، والنماذج المحلية اختيارية. تحديث التطبيق يحافظ على بياناتك؛ الإصدار ما زال تجريبيًا وغير موثّق من Apple.
