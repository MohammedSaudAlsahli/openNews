# OpenStudio 0.2.2 — Experimental preview

Article covers, faster cached images, and clearer source management. Apple Silicon · macOS 14+ · build 203.

## Changes
- Prefer publisher RSS/website covers over inline newsletter branding; remove broken image-link markup and decorative emoji from reader images.
- Cached images no longer wait behind network downloads. Concurrent requests reuse a single download; card and icon decoding uses smaller images to reduce memory.
- Clear Discover and Following tabs, including manual sources; pause, resume, refresh and unsubscribe while retaining archived articles.
- Follow public blog/article listing pages without RSS. RSS is preferred when advertised; supported pages refresh every six hours while the app is running.
- Existing reader content upgrades when opened; manual feed refresh reprocesses cached feed content.

## Update
Use **Check for Updates** in the installed app. Sparkle downloads the signed package and replaces the current app. Saved articles, conversations, drafts and local models stay outside the application bundle. Install in Applications rather than running from the DMG.

## Limitations
Experimental, ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized. macOS 14+ Apple Silicon only. Some websites require JavaScript, block retrieval or provide no image. The image cache is bounded at 100 MiB; evicted images may be downloaded again. No guarantee that every publisher exposes its complete article or cover.

## العربية
تحسين أغلفة المقالات وإزالة الروابط المكسورة حول الصور، وتسريع قراءة الصور المحفوظة وتخفيف استهلاك الذاكرة. إدارة أوضح للمصادر مع الإيقاف وإلغاء المتابعة دون حذف المقالات، ودعم صفحات المدونات العامة التي لا توفر RSS. التحديث يستبدل التطبيق ويحافظ على بياناتك. الإصدار تجريبي وغير موثق من Apple.
